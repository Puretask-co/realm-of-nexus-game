# Code of conduct

## Our standards

We want collaboration to stay **respectful**, **constructive**, and **focused on the project**.

- Use welcoming and inclusive language; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Accept responsibility and apologize when mistakes happen — everyone learns.
- Keep feedback **specific** (what, why, what would help).
- Respect confidentiality of unreleased content or private discussions when applicable.

## Scope

This applies in repository spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and project-related chat where contributors represent the project.

## Reporting

If you see abuse, harassment, or other harmful behavior, contact the maintainers privately. For GitHub-hosted repos, you can also use [GitHub reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) where appropriate.

## Attribution

Inspired by common open-source community norms; adapt as your team grows.
